Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Life Dashboard ("the app") is a personal desktop app that runs on the user's own Windows computer. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it. The short version: your data stays on your computer, and nobody else receives it.

No server, no account, no tracking

  • The app has no backend server. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to any of your data.
  • There is no sign-up and no Life Dashboard account.
  • The app contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar, the app requests the calendar.readonly permission and uses it only to read:

  • the list of your calendars, so you can choose which ones to show, and
  • events on the calendars you choose: title, start and end time, all-day flag, status (for example cancelled), whether the event marks you as busy or free, and your response (for example declined).

How it is used. Events are shown next to your tasks and used to calculate how much free time you have each day. The app never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events.

Where it is stored. Events are cached in an encrypted database on your computer. Google's sign-in tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Sharing. Google user data is not sold, not shared with the developer or any third party, and not used for advertising. It is never used to train AI or machine-learning models. If you choose to include calendar information in an export or in an AI review that you start yourself, it is sent only where you send it, only when you ask, and only for that feature.

Life Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data

Tasks, routines, health entries, goals, and notes you enter are stored only in the encrypted database on your computer. If you connect a bank-data provider (such as SimpleFIN), account balances and transactions are downloaded from that provider to your computer, and the provider's access link is kept in Windows Credential Manager. That provider's own privacy policy covers the data it holds.

Network connections

The app connects only to services you set up: Google Calendar, your bank-data provider, an AI provider if you configure one, and the app's update server. It makes these connections only while it is open.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect in the app: Settings → Calendar → Disconnect revokes the app's Google access and lets you keep or delete the events already cached.
  • Revoke from Google: remove Life Dashboard at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes all stored data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
